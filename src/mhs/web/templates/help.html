What it does

For every visit there are up to three documents: the provider's bill or statement, the insurer's decision (the EOB, explanation of benefits) and your payment, usually from your HSA. My Health Spend lines them up per visit and checks them against each other, so a bill that asks for more than the EOB says you owe, a payment made twice, or a balance nobody asked you about doesn't go unnoticed.

Home, visits and your household

Home starts with the year's totals and how many visits are in each status. Then Needs your attention: the visits that need you, the most dollars first (what's at stake plus what's still owed), then the oldest, each with the reason in a sentence. Then the year's visits as cards: who it was for, the provider and date, its status, how many of the documents it needs have arrived, and the amount at issue, which is the open findings on the visit, counting the same dollars once.

A visit has everything on one page: what's wrong, the bill and the EOB line by line (a line that doesn't agree is highlighted with the difference), who pays what, the documents, and the case history from the visit to today.

Household shows each person's visits and money for the year, and the family's, which is their sum. Showing (on Home, Household and Visits) switches between the family and one person; your choice stays while you move between pages, until you close the browser{% if not path_import %} or sign out{% endif %}.

On a phone the pages are in the bar at the bottom of the screen; Add opens {{ 'Uploads, where Take a photo opens the camera' if not path_import else 'Import' }}.

Signing in

Sign in with your email and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Sessions end after 15 minutes without activity. Five wrong tries lock the account for 15 minutes.

Recovery codes. When you set up your authenticator you get ten recovery codes. Keep them away from your phone. If you forget your password or lose your phone, use one on Forgot your password or lost your phone?: you choose a new password and set up your authenticator again. Your name in the header opens your account page, where you can make new codes.

Uploading bills, EOBs and HSA statements

  1. On Uploads, choose a file and what it is, or let the app decide. On a phone, Take a photo opens the camera and uploads the photo as soon as you take it. PDFs and phone photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC) of bills, statements and EOBs are read for you; HSA and insurer exports (CSV, XLS, XLSX) go straight to a preview.
  2. Names, member IDs, account numbers and other identifiers are replaced with codes before anything is read further; your own pages show the real values.
  3. When the upload says Review, open it. The document is shown above the fields it was read into. Fields marked check were read with less confidence or weren't found: compare them with the document and correct them.
  4. Choose the provider, the patient, the plan and the visit, then confirm. Nothing reaches your visits before you do, and your corrections are kept beside what was read.

The same file twice is refused. A second scan of a document you already have, a re-sent statement or a corrected EOB is pointed out on the review page.

Importing a file

An ingest file is a JSON file in the app's own format (schema/mhs-ingest.schema.json), usually made by the converter from your insurer's claims export, your HSA export and statement PDFs. {% if path_import %}Import it on Import by its path, so the statement PDFs it refers to are stored too.{% else %}Upload it on Import together with the PDFs it refers to.{% endif %} The preview shows what will be added or changed before anything is saved, and a file with errors saves nothing. Changes you made in the app are kept unless you tick "Replace my edits".

A difference under $1.00 is cleared within tolerance and isn't a finding; cleared amounts are added up per provider each year, and a provider past $5.00 is flagged (ADR-015, ADR-035). Balances owed and duplicate charges are never cleared. Every finding is shown with the numbers that disagree, on the visit's page.

HSA flags

The app assumes health spending is paid from your HSA. It flags what you owe that no HSA payment covers: Unpaid: pay from HSA when the provider still shows it owed, and Paid outside the HSA when it was paid some other way, which you may be able to reimburse yourself for from the HSA. An HSA payment with no claim, statement or receipt to match is flagged too. These are possibilities to look into, not rulings on what the HSA allows.

Settings

Plans (with deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums), household members, payment accounts, providers and their other names, and which providers bill together. {% if path_import %}Changes are written to data/settings.json, which the converter reads.{% else %}Download settings.json from Settings for the converter.{% endif %}

Your data

Your data downloads everything as a ZIP you can keep or import again, and deletes your account. If you're the last member of your household, deleting your account deletes all of its data at once.

What it isn't

Information to help you check your bills: not medical, legal, tax or financial advice. Confirm anything you act on with your provider, insurer or HSA administrator.
