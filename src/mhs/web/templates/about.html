Check every bill. Fix what’s wrong. Pay only what you owe.

CareBack’s mission: fair medical bills for everyone, at no cost.

Why I started CareBack

Healthcare is eighteen percent of the American economy, and more of its cost lands on each of us every year. When a bill is wrong, we’re the first line of defense, without the billing experts the insurers have.

I learned that the hard way. Over a series of monthly treatments, the same care was coded and billed differently, and I was paying more. I found it, asked my provider to fix it, and they did. Then I heard the same story from friends and colleagues.

I didn’t need to fix this for myself. It needs fixing, period. That’s why I started CareBack.

When it comes to your medical bills, we’ve got your back.

Don Huovinen Founder, CareBack LinkedIn {{ icon("external_link") }}

How we work

  • {{ icon("heart_handshake") }}A community service, at no cost.

    Free to use, with no fee and no cut of what you get back. If it proves its value, we aim to partner with a leading nonprofit to keep it free.

  • {{ icon("circle_check") }}You stay in charge.

    CareBack checks every bill, EOB and payment, finds where they disagree and drafts what to say. You review it and decide what’s sent.

  • {{ icon("wallet") }}Paying the efficient way.

    We believe your HSA is the most efficient way to pay for care. CareBack shows which expenses it can likely cover and keeps the receipts.

  • {{ icon("code") }}100% open source.

    Our code is public and free for anyone to use. The goal isn’t to make money from a problem many people share. It’s to solve it, for everyone.

Where it’s going

Next, not built yet

Today you review what CareBack finds and send it yourself. Next, with your permission, CareBack will hand its findings to your own AI assistant, or contact the provider or insurer for you, so you don’t have to make the calls.

For developers: through open standards for personal agents, such as A2A, MCP and Poppy.

Build it with us.
