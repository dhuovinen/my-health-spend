Questions you shouldn’t have to answer alone.

One visit produces a bill, an insurance decision and a payment, weeks apart and in different formats, from three parties. No one reconciles them.

  • Was the provider in network, and was I billed that way?
  • Did the insurer process the claim, and does what I owe match?
  • Am I overpaying through a wrong rate, a duplicate charge or a misapplied copay?
  • Was it paid from the right account, and is anything still outstanding?

Here’s what CareBack sees that nobody else checks.

One visit, three stories: your provider, your insurer and your payment account each keep their own record of it. CareBack puts them side by side.

How we fight fair

On your side. Careful with the facts.

Standing up to a billing office only works if you’re right. So CareBack shows its work.

  • {{ icon("search") }}Every finding shows its evidence.

    The amounts, the lines and the documents they came from, side by side.

About us

Why I started CareBack.

Over a series of monthly treatments, the same care was coded and billed differently, and I was paying more. I got it fixed, then heard the same story from friends and colleagues. It needs fixing, period. CareBack is free, open source and run as a community service.

Don Huovinen, founder Read our story {{ icon("arrow_right") }}

Straight answers.

Still wondering about something? About us has the longer story.

Will CareBack get my money back?{{ icon("plus") }}

It can’t promise that, and no one honest can. It finds where your bill, your EOB and your payments disagree, shows you the evidence, and helps you ask the provider or insurer about it. Whether money comes back is up to their review.

Who is behind CareBack?{{ icon("plus") }}

Don Huovinen, who started it after finding billing errors in his own care. CareBack is a free community service and open source. Read our story.

Is this health insurance?{{ icon("plus") }}

No. CareBack helps individuals and their households understand their health spending. It isn’t insurance, a payment account or a replacement for your insurer.

Can it help with bills I’ve already paid?{{ icon("plus") }}

Yes, that’s where it starts. Discrepancies can show up even after a bill has been paid.
