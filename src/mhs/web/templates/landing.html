How we fight fair

On your side. Careful with the facts.

Standing up to a billing office only works if you’re right. So CareBack shows its work.

  • {{ icon("search") }}Every finding shows its evidence.

    The amounts, the lines and the documents they came from, side by side.

  • {{ icon("circle_help") }}A question until it’s confirmed.

    “Potential discrepancy”, never “error”, until the provider or insurer agrees.

  • {{ icon("lock") }}Your identity stays out of it.

    Names, member numbers and dates are removed or shifted before any document is read by AI.

  • {{ icon("download") }}Your data leaves with you.

    Export everything or delete it, any time, from your account.

Questions you shouldn’t have to answer alone.

One visit produces a bill, an insurance decision and a payment, weeks apart and in different formats, from three parties. No one reconciles them.

  • Was the provider in network, and was I billed that way?
  • Did the insurer process the claim, and does what I owe match?
  • Am I overpaying through a wrong rate, a duplicate charge or a misapplied copay?
  • Was it paid from the right account, and is anything still outstanding?
About us

Check every bill. Fix what’s wrong. Pay only what you owe.

Healthcare is eighteen percent of the American economy, and more of its cost lands on each of us every year. When a bill is wrong, we’re the first line of defense, without the billing experts the insurers have.

I learned that the hard way. Over a series of monthly treatments, the same care was coded and billed differently, and I was paying more. I found it, asked my provider to fix it, and they did. Then I heard the same story from friends and colleagues. I didn’t need to fix this for myself. It needs fixing, period. That’s why I started CareBack.

When it comes to your medical bills, we’ve got your back.

How we work

  • {{ icon("heart_handshake") }}
    A community service, at no cost.

    Lots of companies help with medical bills for a fee or a cut of the savings. That isn’t us: CareBack is free to use. If it proves its value, our aim is to partner with a leading nonprofit to keep providing it at no cost.

  • {{ icon("bot") }}
    Advocates that work for you.

    CareBack checks every bill, EOB and payment, finds where they disagree and drafts what to say. Today you review it and send it. Next, with your permission, CareBack will hand its findings to your own AI agent, or act for you directly, through open standards for personal agents such as A2A, MCP and Poppy.

  • {{ icon("wallet") }}
    Paying the efficient way.

    We believe your HSA is the most efficient way to pay for care. CareBack shows which expenses it can likely cover and keeps the receipts, so you pay for your care in the most efficient way possible.

  • {{ icon("code") }}
    100% open source.

    Our code is public and free for anyone to use. Our goal isn’t to make money from a problem many people share. It’s to solve it, for everyone. If you run a nonprofit that can do this better or reach more people, I’d be glad to work with you, support you or follow your lead. It isn’t about me. It’s about the outcome.

Straight answers.

Will CareBack get my money back?{{ icon("plus") }}

It can’t promise that, and no one honest can. It finds where your bill, your EOB and your payments disagree, shows you the evidence, and helps you ask the provider or insurer about it. Whether money comes back is up to their review.

Who is behind CareBack?{{ icon("plus") }}

Don Huovinen, who started it after finding billing errors in his own care. CareBack is a free community service and open source. Read more about us.

Is this health insurance?{{ icon("plus") }}

No. CareBack helps individuals and their households understand their health spending. It isn’t insurance, a payment account or a replacement for your insurer.

Can it help with bills I’ve already paid?{{ icon("plus") }}

Yes, that’s where it starts. Discrepancies can show up even after a bill has been paid.
