You shouldn’t have to be
a billing expert to pay fairly.

One visit produces a bill, an insurance decision and a payment, often weeks apart and in different formats. So these questions go unanswered:

  • Was the provider in network, and was I billed that way?
  • Did the insurer process the claim, and does what I owe match?
  • Am I overpaying through a wrong rate, a duplicate charge or a misapplied copay?
  • Was it paid from the right account, and is anything still outstanding?
Good questions. Clear answers.

A little less unknown.

Because understanding your healthcare spending shouldn’t come with more question marks.

What does My Health $pend look for?{{ icon("plus") }}

It compares your provider bill, insurance explanation of benefits (EOB) and payment records for the same visit. That can help surface duplicate charges, mismatched patient balances, network-rate questions and payments that haven’t been credited.

Is this health insurance?{{ icon("plus") }}

No. My Health $pend helps individuals understand their healthcare spending. It is not health insurance, a payment account or a replacement for your insurer.

Can it help with bills I’ve already paid?{{ icon("plus") }}

Yes, that is where it starts. Looking back across bills, insurance decisions and payments may reveal discrepancies even after a bill has been paid. Any refund depends on review and resolution with the provider or insurer.

What about my next appointment?{{ icon("plus") }}

The next step in our vision is helping you compare expected insurance costs with available self-pay prices before a visit. Deductibles, network status and whether a payment counts toward your plan limits all matter.
