Pattern: **consumer players cluster at two ends.** Bill review/negotiation (HCC FIRST, Goodbill) works
per bill on facility bills before payment. HSA trackers work on receipts and reimbursements with no view
of claims or bills. Nobody found in this pass joins claims, bills, payments and the HSA across a plan
year and a household.
