# Competitor research: HCC FIRST (hccfirst.com)

Researched 2026-10-10 for My Health Spend (MH$).

| File | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [site-documentation.md](site-documentation.md) | Every public page: offer, pricing, workflow, FAQ, learning modules, the "Shop Services" preview, employer page, signup, legal pages |
| [ux-and-design.md](ux-and-design.md) | Information architecture, visual design, interaction patterns, tone of voice, mobile, trust devices, tech stack |
| [company-and-market.md](company-and-market.md) | Company facts, what outside sources do and don't confirm, adjacent competitors |
| [analysis.md](analysis.md) | Their focus vs. MH$'s, overlap and gaps, and opportunities for differentiated value |

## Method

- Pages enumerated from `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` (15 URLs), plus two linked pages not in the sitemap
  (`/shop-for-services`, `/login`). Each page's server-rendered HTML was read in full.
- Interactive pages were driven in a browser: every tap target on the "Anatomy" modules, all five steps of
  the Shop Services walkthrough, and the quiz and slider data embedded in the page payload.
- Signup and sign-in pages were viewed but **no account was created**, so nothing behind the login (the
  "vault", the review output, the members-only "Behind the Bill" game) was seen. Statements about the
  logged-in product come from the marketing pages, FAQ and Terms.
- Outside sources: web search (company, founder, press, competitors) and a WHOIS lookup.

## Notes on this folder

- Their content is summarized and described here, not copied. Short quotes are marked as such. Prices,
  plan names, figures and structural facts are recorded as stated on the site on 2026-10-10.
- Sites change. Re-check pricing and claims before relying on them.
