More and more of the bill is landing on us. If you buy your own coverage on the ACA Marketplace, the average deductible went from about $2,760 in 2025 to about $3,790 in 2026. That’s up thirty-seven percent in a single year. (Marketplace plans only. Don’t present it as the average for all insurance.)
