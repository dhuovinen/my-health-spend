I didn’t realize how broken this was until I had a chronic health problem to deal with. Your annual checkup? They probably get that right. Anything more complex, and in my experience the mistakes start to show up. I was having a monthly series of eye injections for my vision. Same treatment every month. But what was coded, and what I was billed, wasn’t the same. We’d changed insurers at the end of 2025, and at five to six hundred dollars a visit, every month, I knew 2026 was going to hit my deductible hard. So I went back through the earlier charges and projected the rest of the year. The bills weren’t consistent. When I drilled in, they really weren’t, and I was paying more. I used AI to find the outliers. Knowing the treatment was the same, I went back to my provider and had the visits reclassified: re-coded to match what was actually done. That saved me hundreds of dollars.
