The obvious ones get noticed. The quiet ones get paid.

Then I started asking around. A colleague went to the ER and was given a pain reliever. The bill was over four thousand dollars, because it was coded as an ambulance service. When a mistake is that egregious, at least you notice it. Most aren’t. Recently my provider couldn’t give me an itemized bill, just a statement saying I owed three hundred fifteen dollars. My insurer rejected the first claim; once it was resubmitted, it confirmed I owed the negotiated rate: two hundred seventy-five. The provider never got the message. They kept asking for $315 by mail, email and phone, when I’d already paid. I had to show them the right amount and ask for a refund.
