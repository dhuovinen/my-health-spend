That’s when I realized I didn’t need to fix this for myself. It needs fixing, period. That’s why I started CareBack. Here’s what it does, on a made-up visit. Your provider says you owe $348. Your insurer’s explanation of benefits says you owe $198, and that’s what you paid from your HSA. CareBack lines the three up and circles the $150 gap. It doesn’t call it an error; it says it’s worth a closer look, shows you the documents, and drafts the letter if you want to ask. You decide what’s sent. (The example is fictional; say so.)
