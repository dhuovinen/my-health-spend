So what exists today? CareBack is built and running. You can upload or forward your bills, EOBs and HSA exports. It matches every visit line by line and gives each one a status. It removes names and member numbers before any AI reads a document. It drafts the letter, and you decide what’s sent. It’s Python, FastAPI and Postgres, MIT licensed, and live at carebackhealth.com. And it’s free. No fee, no cut of what you get back. What’s next is where you come in. Developers: the hardest problems are reading documents accurately and matching them to the right visit. Nonprofits: you already serve the households who need this most, and you know what they need.
